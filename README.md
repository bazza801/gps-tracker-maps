# Corrie to Cairn map packages

Offline regional map packages for Corrie to Cairn, a walking and hill navigation app. Each package is a single vector
MBTiles file that the app downloads (or imports) and shows without a network connection.

The packages and a `catalogue.json` describing them are attached to this repository's
[Releases](https://github.com/bazza801/gps-tracker-maps/releases); nothing large is committed here.

## Regions

Scotland, England, Wales, Ireland (North and South) and the Isle of Man are covered by overlapping regions (neighbours overlap by a few kilometres, so a walk near an edge is on one map), and by larger whole-area maps (All of Scotland, Northern England, All of Wales, Southern England and All of Ireland). Every hill on the app's lists is on at least one regional map. Download only the ones you need.

Packages are rebuilt roughly twice a year, or when Ordnance Survey or the other sources publish new data. A release may
link to unchanged packages attached to an earlier release (its notes say which), so earlier releases are
kept while they're in use. Each package records its Maps page heading, and `catalogue.json` lists the
headings in order, so new regions appear in the app without an app update.

## What's in a package

Great Britain (Scotland, England and Wales):

- **OS Open Zoomstack**: sea, lochs, rivers and streams, woodland, green spaces, towns, buildings, roads, railways and place names.
- **OS Terrain 50**: 10 m contours and spot heights.
- **OpenStreetMap**: walking paths, tracks, bridleways and steps.

Ireland and the Isle of Man (Ordnance Survey data covers Great Britain only):

- **OpenStreetMap**: the coastline, sea, lakes, rivers and streams, woodland, green spaces, towns, buildings, roads,
  railways, place names, peaks with heights, and walking paths, tracks, bridleways and steps.
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-30**: 10 m contours, made from the 30 m elevation model.

Zoom levels 9 to 14. Using a package outside the app: it's a standard MBTiles file with
Mapbox Vector Tile (PBF) tiles, readable by MapLibre and other vector tile tools.

## Place names (search)

`places.sqlite.gz` is the app's optional place search file: about 310,000 named places across Great
Britain, Ireland and the Isle of Man (towns, villages, hills, glens, corries, passes, lochs and lakes,
rivers and burns, woods, waterfalls, islands, stations, national parks), one file for everywhere. It's a
gzipped SQLite database with a full-text index, listed in `catalogue.json` as `search`.

- Great Britain: **OS Open Names** and **OS Open Rivers**.
- Ireland and the Isle of Man: **OpenStreetMap**.
