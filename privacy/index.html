Corrie to Cairn: privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Corrie to Cairn is an offline hillwalking app for recording walks, following routes, using offline maps and ticking off hill lists. It is made by SlaughSentry. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Everything you record stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data.
  • The app only uses the internet to download maps, and when you choose to open a weather, walk or hill link in your browser.

Your location

The app uses your phone's GPS (only while the app is in use, or while you're recording a walk) to:

  • show where you are on the map;
  • record the walks you choose to record;
  • follow a route and warn you if you go off it;
  • work out which listed hills a walk reached;
  • suggest a detailed map for the area you're in or looking at.

Your location is processed and stored only on your phone. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else, except as described under "Links you open" below.

While you record a walk, the app shows a notification and keeps recording with the screen off. Recording stops when you stop it.

What's stored on your phone

  • Walks you record (positions, times, heights, names), routes you import (GPX files), and the hills you've ticked off.
  • Downloaded map files.
  • Your settings.

This stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it (Android may offer to keep the app's data when you uninstall; that copy also stays on your phone).

Backups

  • Backups you make: "Back up" creates a file with your walks and ticks. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, OneDrive, email or a folder on the phone). The developer never receives it.
  • Android's own backup: if you have Android backup switched on, Android may copy the app's walks, ticks, routes and settings to your Google account, as it does for other apps. This is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the developer cannot see it. Downloaded maps aren't included.

Internet use

  • Maps: the list of downloadable maps and the map files themselves are downloaded from GitHub (github.com, run by GitHub, Inc.). Like any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address when you download; no other information is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links you open: weather, walk and hill links open in your web browser, only when you tap them. Some of these links include a location:
    • the Met Office forecast for a spot, and Komoot walks near a spot, include the position you asked about (your position, the middle of the map, or a hill's summit);
    • mountain area forecasts (MWIS, Met Office), Walkhighlands, Walklakes and hill-bagging.co.uk links identify an area or a hill, not you. Once open, those websites' own privacy policies apply.

Nothing else is sent from the app.

Permissions

  • Location (precise): for the map position, recording and route following, as above.
  • Notifications: to show that a walk is recording (with a Stop button) and off-route warnings on the lock screen.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep recording a walk you started while the screen is off.
  • Vibration: for off-route warnings.
  • Internet: for map downloads only.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
